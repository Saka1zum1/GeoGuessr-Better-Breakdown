# GeoGuessr Better Breakdown

After you guess, see **where you were**, **where you clicked**, and **what it actually looked like** — all on the results map, without jumping to Google Maps.

Works on **Classic**, **Daily Challenge**, **Duels**, and **Team Duels** when the round or game summary is on screen.

## What you get

- **Pin previews** — Hover your guess or the correct pin for a Street View thumbnail and how far the nearest coverage is.
- **In-page Street View** — Click a pin to open a split Street View panel beside the map.
- **Round-by-round focus** — In Classic finals, click a round in the list to zoom the map to that guess and answer.
- **Coverage overlay** — Turn on the blue Street View layer to spot where you *could* have checked before guessing.
- **Path on the map** — While you move in Street View, your route draws on the guess map (great for reviewing movement).
- **Older imagery** — Pick another capture date from the dropdown when multiple Street View versions exist.
- **Handy Street View tools** — Screenshot, copy link, open in Google Maps, jump forward/back, photo mode (hide UI for a clean shot).
- **Duel extras** — Rounds panel, resizable map, and the same Street View controls on duel breakdown.
- **Save to Map-Making** — Optional: save the spot to [map-making.app](https://map-making.app) (you’ll need your own API key once).

## Map types & map tools

On the results map you can switch roadmap / satellite / terrain and use the other overlay buttons the script adds along the top of the map.

## Map-Making (optional)

1. Open Street View from a pin.
2. Tap **Save** (upload icon).
3. Paste your API key the first time — create one at [map-making.app/keys](https://map-making.app/keys).

## Tips players use

- End of round → hover pins before opening full Street View.
- Green pin = true location; your pin = what you locked in.
- Duel summary → use the list control on the map to hop between rounds.
- Photo mode → camera button in Street View, **Esc** to leave.
